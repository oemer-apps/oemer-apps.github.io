2. Overview

I process as little personal data as possible. Most of my apps store their data (e.g. game progress, settings, entries) only locally on your device. I never sell data. Not every app uses every service described below. Each section names the apps it applies to, and each app's "Data safety" section on Google Play lists the data that app collects.

3. This website

3.1 Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub processes the technical data needed to deliver it, such as your IP address, the time of access, the page requested and browser information (server log files). The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running the website reliably and securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's privacy statement.

3.2 No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no external fonts or scripts. If you switch the language, only your choice ("de" or "en") is stored in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to me.

3.3 Contact by email

If you email me, I process your email address and your message in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed, unless the law requires me to keep them. I use Gmail by Google Ireland Limited for email.

4. Apps and games

4.1 Download via Google Play

My apps are distributed through Google Play. Google processes data such as your account and device information on its own responsibility when you download or update an app; see Google's Privacy Policy. From Google Play I receive only aggregated, anonymous statistics (e.g. install numbers and crash reports that don't identify you).

4.2 Local storage

Game progress, settings, high scores and your entries are stored only on your device and are not sent to me unless stated otherwise below. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes this data.

4.3 Advertising with Google AdMob

Some apps and games are funded by ads served through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. Google uses this data to deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalise ads.

On first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. You only see personalised ads if you consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Without consent, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app's settings, and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4.4 In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. Only Google receives your payment details. I receive only what is needed to unlock your purchase: the product, an order number and the purchase status (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Tax and commercial law require me to keep purchase records for up to 10 years (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

4.5 Online features with Google Firebase (e.g. Miles N Us)

Apps with online features, such as connecting with your partner in Miles N Us, use Google Firebase, provided by Google Ireland Limited as my data processor under a data processing agreement. The database is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany (region europe-west3). Firebase processes:

  • Sign-in (Firebase Authentication): a random user ID and, if you sign in with an email address or Google account, those sign-in details;
  • Content (Cloud Firestore): what you enter and share with your partner, e.g. your display name, your answers to daily questions, game progress, dates such as your anniversary or next meeting and, if you use the distance calculator, the city or country you enter;
  • Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging): a device token, so you can be notified when your partner has answered.

Only the person you connected with by code can see what you share. The legal basis is providing the app features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Android asks your permission before the app can send notifications. In individual cases data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses also apply. Your data is kept until you delete your connection or account (see section 5).

4.6 Open Food Facts product database (e.g. Frisch, Makros)

When you scan a barcode or search for a product in one of my nutrition or household apps, the app sends the barcode or search term to the open database Open Food Facts (Saint-Maur-des-Fossés, France) to fetch product information. Your IP address is necessarily sent as part of this request. Your personal entries, goals or body data are not sent; they stay on your device (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). See Open Food Facts privacy policy.

4.7 Device permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan barcodes. The camera image is analysed only on your device and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: used for reminders and alerts, e.g. when your partner has answered. You can turn them off in Android settings at any time.
  • Accessibility service (e.g. Atempause): apps that help you use your phone more mindfully need this permission to detect which app is being opened and show a pause screen. They read only the name of the opened app, and only on your device. They never read, store or send screen content, input or messages. You grant this permission yourself in Android settings and can revoke it there at any time.

5. Deleting your data

To delete locally stored data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

For data stored on the server by online features (e.g. Miles N Us), use the in-app option to delete your account or connection where available. You can also email oemer.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and the name of the app. I will delete your account and all related data within 30 days, except data I am legally required to keep (e.g. purchase records).

6. Children

My apps are not specifically directed at children under 16, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, please contact me so I can delete it.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You may also withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). A short email to oemer.apps@gmail.com is enough. You can also complain to a data protection authority. My authority is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany, www.datenschutz-berlin.de.

8. Changes

I will update this policy when new apps or features are added or when the law changes. The current version on this page always applies.
